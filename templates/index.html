About & Privacy Architecture

How ClauseLens protects your sensitive legal agreements.

Privacy & Data Storage Guarantee

ClauseLens is built from the ground up to keep your documents private and secure:

  • Local Storage Only: All database persistence uses SQLite on your local machine. No contracts are stored in any cloud service or remote repository.
  • Temporary Upload Files: Contract uploads are written to a private temporary directory with randomized filenames, parsed in memory, and immediately deleted in a guaranteed finally block.
  • Zero Cloud AI by Default: Core clause detection, segmentation, and policy matching are 100% deterministic and rule-based. No text leaves your machine unless you specifically opt-in to Nemotron.
  • Automatic Retention Purge: Analyses expire automatically after 30 days (RETENTION_DAYS) and are wiped on startup. You can also purge or delete records manually anytime.

Policy Assistance, Not Legal Advice

Notice: ClauseLens provides policy-based assistance and is not legal advice. It checks only the supported clause types against the policies you configured. The absence of a flagged issue does not mean a contract is safe or complete. Consult a qualified professional before signing.

ClauseLens never declares a contract "safe", "legal", or "illegal". It only compares contract clauses against your configured rules and reports evidence-backed findings.
